Profile

I work on one problem from several sides: getting from raw sequencing data to answers people can use, including where computing power and connectivity are scarce.

That means new assembly algorithms, reproducible pipelines on the Linux/HPC systems I have run for five years, and software that reaches researchers and health workers by phone, built alongside wet‑lab scientists. Training is part of the job too: I have mentored 100+ students and researchers in reproducible computational biology.
