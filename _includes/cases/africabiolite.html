AfricaBioLite

Most genomic resources assume a broadband connection and a browser. AfricaBioLite lets a researcher, plant breeder or clinician query them by SMS, USSD or WhatsApp, from any phone with a mobile signal.

What you can ask it

A user sends a short command, such as VAR HUMAN rs113488022, PROT PLASMODIUM KELCH13 or ENA MPOX DRC, or walks a USSD menu. Answers come back as condensed text, an ASCII genome track for feature phones, or a compressed track image, for 15 species from human to cassava, yam, cocoa and Plasmodium.

Variants. Location, predicted effect per allele from Ensembl VEP on the MANE transcript, SIFT/PolyPhen scores and ClinVar significance.
Proteins. UniProt (Swiss‑Prot first), InterPro, Pfam and PROSITE domains, PDB count and AlphaFold model. Only UniProt entries that Ensembl links to the gene are used, so a free‑text match can't return an unrelated protein.
African populations. Allele frequencies across seven African 1000 Genomes populations and the AFR super‑population, Fst differentiation, and Africa‑enriched variants in a region.
Sequencing activity. Public runs, samples, recent submissions and the most active studies for pathogens, vectors and crops, by country or Africa‑wide. Country matching is exact, so Niger is never counted as Nigeria.
Genes and regions. Gene detail, region maps, sequence, UCSC tracks, orthologs, disease associations and GTEx tissue expression.
Trait & drug‑resistance loci. Curated loci such as kelch13, crt, HBB, G6PD, APOL1 and sorghum LGS1, shown only after Ensembl verifies them on the current assembly.

How it's built

One WSGI app for every channel. USSD, SMS and the WhatsApp Cloud API share one app; stateless USSD sessions replay through a menu state machine, so it scales across gunicorn workers.
USSD never waits on a slow API. Queries run on a Redis/RQ job queue. A fast answer appears on screen; a slow one ends the session with a reference number and arrives by SMS.
Neural image compression. A MobileNet encoder squeezes a genome‑track image into a ~2 KB latent, sent as SMS packets and decoded on the other end; runs on a Raspberry Pi with a USB GSM modem.
Offline‑first cache. SQLite with per‑data‑type expiry and prefetch of key loci over Wi‑Fi.
Privacy and safety. Phone numbers are stored only as keyed pseudonyms, secrets come only from the environment, and clinical results carry a disclaimer.
Languages, reviewed before release. English is live; Swahili, French, Hausa, Yoruba, Amharic, Twi, Arabic and Portuguese are offered once a native speaker signs them off. A translation that breaks menu numbering or the 182‑character USSD limit falls back to English.

Keeping it correct

Upstream databases rename species and replace assemblies. A weekly CI job runs real queries end to end, rebuilds the species catalogue (assemblies and chromosome‑name maps) from Ensembl, and re‑verifies every trait locus by gene ID.
