BioLearn Hub

A teaching and training platform for bioinformatics and biomedical data science, where students practise on the same tools they'll use in research.

It reuses Moodle's data‑model design (roles and capabilities, enrolment, gradebook, course completion) rather than Moodle itself. Lecturers author Program → Course → Module → Lesson content with drip release, staff preview and version history, and manage cohorts, rosters and one gradebook. Grading, enrolment and role changes go to an audit log.

Practice runs through a Rosalind‑style skill tree: student code executes in sandboxed containers, with runner images for Python, general NGS work, hifiasm, prokka, bakta and variant calling.
