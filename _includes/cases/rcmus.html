rcMUS framework

The theory underneath vMUS‑dBG: Read‑Consistent Minimum Unique Substrings.

A parameter‑free, linear‑time framework for representing genomic sequence. It replaces the user‑chosen k of k‑mer methods with substrings whose length is set by the data, and applies to scalable indexing of metagenomic reference databases as well as assembly.
