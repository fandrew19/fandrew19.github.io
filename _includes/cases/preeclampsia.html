Community health workers on feature phones

Preeclampsia screening system

A community health worker dials a short code, answers a few clinical questions, and the patient gets a risk tier with aspirin and referral guidance, then automated SMS reminders.

A rule‑based risk engine does the scoring, an agentic escalation state machine handles emergency triage, and an offline‑first Progressive Web App gives health workers the same scoring pipeline without a connection. Contacts are held in an encrypted, consent‑tracked registry with audit logging and JWT role‑based admin access.

The LLM is kept on a short leash: it rephrases messages and classifies free‑text symptoms, and never makes a clinical decision.
