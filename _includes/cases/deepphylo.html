DeepPhylo

Bacterial phylogenetics from raw paired reads to a tree, with an alignment‑free route that uses a protein language model.

An end‑to‑end Nextflow pipeline: fastp QC, SPAdes assembly and QUAST, CDS prediction, and eggNOG‑mapper functional annotation (GO, KEGG, COG, EC). The tree can be built two ways: classical MAFFT + IQ‑TREE2 maximum likelihood, or Neighbor‑Joining from ESM‑2 embedding distances with no alignment at all.

It also includes an experimental CNN‑Transformer CDS boundary detector that can be trained on any annotated reference.
