African DSI DataBank

Federated infrastructure for Africa's genomic and biodiversity data.

A federated databank of African‑origin digital sequence information: genomes, proteins, eDNA, metagenomes, protein structures and One Health data. My part is the data itself: finding record inconsistencies, recommending metadata improvements, and supporting quality assurance and standardisation. Co‑author of the Nature Reviews Genetics (2026) paper describing the infrastructure.
