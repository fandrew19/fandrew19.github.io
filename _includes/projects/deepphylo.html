DeepPhylo

AI‑augmented bacterial genome phylogenetics pipeline
  • End‑to‑end Nextflow pipeline taking paired Illumina reads through fastp QC, SPAdes assembly and QUAST, CDS prediction, eggNOG‑mapper functional annotation (GO / KEGG / COG / EC) and phylogenetic inference.
  • Two tree routes: classical MAFFT + IQ‑TREE2 maximum likelihood, or an alignment‑free route building Neighbor‑Joining trees from ESM‑2 protein‑language‑model embedding distances.
  • Experimental CNN‑Transformer CDS boundary detector trainable on any annotated reference; runs on SLURM with Singularity and reports via MultiQC.
