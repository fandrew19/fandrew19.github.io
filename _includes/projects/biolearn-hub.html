BioLearn Hub

Teaching & training platform for bioinformatics and biomedical data science · Developer
  • Custom learning platform for course delivery and auto‑graded hands‑on practice, reusing Moodle's data‑model design (roles and capabilities, enrolment, gradebook, course completion) instead of running Moodle itself.
