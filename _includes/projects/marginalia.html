AI Exam Script Marking — criterion‑by‑criterion marking of handwritten and typed scripts · Developer
  • Full‑stack system that takes scanned handwriting, typed documents or source code, splits booklets into per‑question answers, and drafts a mark against one of seven rubric types (analytic, holistic, calculation, open, report, essay, code).
  • As much marking as possible is plain Python, not a model's judgement: final numeric answers, units and significant figures are checked symbolically, and code is executed against the teacher's tests in an isolated container. A remark gives the same numbers and uses no AI quota.
  • Every mark is a draft: anything unverified is held for review, and teachers approve before release. Weighted course grades keep provisional and final scores apart and never treat a missing mark as zero.
