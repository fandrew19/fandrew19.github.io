Preeclampsia screening system

Agentic AI risk screening for feature‑phone users in resource‑constrained African settings
  • A community health worker dials a short code, answers a few clinical questions, and the patient receives a risk tier plus aspirin/referral guidance, followed by automated SMS reminders.
  • Rule‑based risk engine, agentic escalation state machine for emergency triage, and an offline‑first Progressive Web App for CHWs sharing the same scoring pipeline.
  • Encrypted, consent‑tracked contact registry with audit logging, JWT role‑based admin auth and Alembic migrations; an LLM is scoped to rephrasing messages and classifying free‑text symptoms, never to clinical decisions. 140 passing tests.
